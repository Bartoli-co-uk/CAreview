# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or level of technical confidence.

## Expected behaviour

- Be constructive, specific, and patient.
- Critique ideas and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Acknowledge uncertainty, correct mistakes, and avoid presenting model output as unquestionable authority.
- Accept reasonable maintainer decisions about scope, safety, and community health.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruption, doxxing, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository discussions and other project spaces, and when someone is publicly representing the project. Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner or maintainers. Do not use a public issue when doing so could expose the reporter or another person.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, issue warnings, restrict participation, or ban participants according to the context and severity. Reports will be handled as privately and fairly as practical; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised.
